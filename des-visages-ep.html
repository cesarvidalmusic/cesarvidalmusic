Des Visages

Des Visages is an electroacoustic duo formed by saxophonist César Vidal and electronic musician Jorge Care. The project explores the interaction between acoustic improvisation and live electronic processing.

Their first EP, Des Visages, was recorded at the Haute école de musique de Genève and develops a dialogue between saxophone, electronics, improvisation and real-time sound transformation.
