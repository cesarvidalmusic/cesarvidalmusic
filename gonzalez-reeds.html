Gonzalez Reeds

official reed partner

César Vidal proudly plays Gonzalez Reeds, the Argentine reed maker based in Mendoza whose work is rooted in carefully cultivated Arundo Donax cane and a precise process of selection, cutting, measuring and finishing. Their reeds are produced in Argentina, at the foot of the Andes, with a strong connection to the land where the cane is grown.

Gonzalez Reeds supports César’s work as a saxophonist, improviser and composer. He has played their reeds for several years, and among their different cuts, Local Jazz 627 and Malbec have become his preferred models for alto saxophone.

“I have played Gonzalez Reeds for several years, and I genuinely trust their quality. Both Local Jazz 627 and Malbec give me the flexibility I need for jazz, experimental music and other situations where the saxophone has to move quickly between different sound worlds. Their response feels direct, open and consistent. I am also drawn to the fact that they are made in Mendoza, Argentina: a Latin American product with a level of craft, reliability and refinement that stands confidently beside the most established reed makers I have used in Europe.”
